Most page owners in Bangladesh start with the **Boost** button. It is right there under the post, it takes two minutes, and the post gets more reach. So why do agencies keep telling you to stop?

## What Boost does well

Boost is fine when you want more people to **see** a post. An offer announcement, a new arrival, a holiday greeting. If the goal is reach or engagement, Boost does the job.

## Where Boost falls short

The problem starts when you want **orders or messages from buyers**, not just likes.

- You get only a few objective choices, so Meta often shows your post to people who like and comment but rarely buy.
- You cannot properly test two creatives against each other.
- You cannot use your Pixel data well, so you cannot target people who visited your site or added to cart.
- Reporting is basic, so it is hard to know your real cost per order.

## What Ads Manager gives you

In Ads Manager you choose the exact goal (sales, leads, messages), split your budget across audiences, test several creatives at once and retarget people who already showed interest. You also see cost per result in one place, so you can stop what is not working.

## A simple rule

- **Want reach or likes?** Boost is okay.
- **Want sales, leads or WhatsApp/Messenger orders?** Use Ads Manager, with the Pixel set up first.
