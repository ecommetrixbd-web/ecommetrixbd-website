A shoot day goes much better when a few things are ready before the camera comes out. Here is the list we share with our clients.

## 1. Know what the video is for

Is it a Facebook ad, a Reel, a product page video or a TVC? Each one needs a different length and frame. Tell us where it will run, and we will plan the cuts (9:16, 4:5 or 16:9) from the start.

## 2. Pick one main message

One video, one message. "Free delivery inside Dhaka" or "lasts 8 hours on one charge" works better than five features in 30 seconds.

## 3. Get the products ready

- Clean, unused samples with no scratches or dust
- Every colour or size you want to show
- Packaging, if the unboxing matters to your buyers

## 4. Decide on people

Will you appear yourself, use a model, or show only hands and product? If a person is speaking, share the main points a day early so they are comfortable on camera.

## 5. Share your brand basics

Logo files, brand colours, fonts and any old videos you liked or disliked. This saves a lot of back and forth in editing.

## 6. Plan the offer

If the video will be an ad, the offer (price, discount, delivery time) should be final before the shoot. Changing it later often means re-editing.

Send us these before the shoot day and we can plan the shots properly. If you are not sure where to start, message us and we will help you work it out.
